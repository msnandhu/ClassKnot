About ClassKnot

From registers and receipts to one connected school.

ClassKnot is school management software built for Indian schools. Most schools still run on paper files, cash counters and busy group chats. ClassKnot replaces them with a single digital ecosystem. Management, teachers, students and parents all work from the same live records, so a mark entered in class reaches the parent's phone and the principal's report at the same moment.
